House rules

{% if accepted %}You've accepted these rules.{% else %}Please read these before you start. It takes two minutes.{% endif %}

Be a good colleague

  • Be kind and respectful. No harassment, bullying, discrimination or personal attacks.
  • Only post photos of colleagues who are happy to be in them.
  • Keep it work-appropriate: nothing offensive, explicit or illegal.
  • No selling, political campaigning or chain messages.

Keep company information safe

  • Everyone in the company can see what you post, so don't share confidential, client or financial information.
  • Never post passwords, personal details of customers, or health information about anyone.

How your information is used

  • Your name, job title, photo and posts are visible to everyone in the company.
  • Moderators can hide posts that break these rules, and HR may review reported content.
  • When you delete a post or comment it disappears straight away, but it is kept for 30 days in case it's needed for a complaint, then removed for good.
  • Sign-ins and moderation actions are logged for security.
  • You can download a copy of your data at any time.
